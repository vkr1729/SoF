👨‍👩‍👦 Parent & Caregiver Quick Guide

Welcome! This platform is designed specifically for your child in Class 2 preparing for the Science Olympiad Foundation (SOF) exams in IGKO, IMO, and NSO.

1. Daily Practice (10 Questions at a time)

Go to the Adventure Missions tab, select a subject, and click on any Set. Each set has 10 unmixed questions. Click the 🔊 Read button on any question if your child needs audio assistance.

2. Overcoming Mistakes

Any question answered incorrectly is automatically stored in the Mistake Bank. Sit with your child once or twice a week to hit "Retry & Master".

3. Weekend Mock Exams

In the SOF Exam Simulator tab, launch the Quick 10-Q Mock or the Full 35-Q Mock to simulate real exam-room conditions with the official TCS iON layout.

4. Resetting Data
