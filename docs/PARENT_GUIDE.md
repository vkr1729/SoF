# 🌟 SOF Olympiad (Class 2) — Parent Quick-Start Guide
### *A 10-Minute Daily Routine for IGKO, IMO & NSO Success*

Dear Parent,

This learning portal was built specifically for **Class 2 students (~7 years old)** preparing for the **SOF Olympiads**:
- 🌍 **IGKO** (International General Knowledge Olympiad)
- 📐 **IMO** (International Mathematics Olympiad)
- 🔬 **NSO** (National Science Olympiad)

It is themed around **Minecraft** (Steve, Alex, Axolotl, Emeralds, and Hearts) to keep practice fun, encouraging, and pressure-free!

---

## 📱 How to Open the App
1. **On Laptop / Tablet / iPad / Phone**:
   - Open your web browser (Chrome, Edge, Safari).
   - Double-click or open `index.html` (or click the live web link if hosted on GitHub Pages/Vercel).
2. **No login or password is required!** All progress, stars, and mistake history save automatically on your device.

---

## 🗓️ Recommended Daily Routine (10–15 Minutes)

### Step 1: Pick One Subject Per Day
- **Monday & Thursday**: 📐 **IMO (Math)**
- **Tuesday & Friday**: 🔬 **NSO (Science)**
- **Wednesday & Saturday**: 🌍 **IGKO (General Knowledge)**
- **Sunday**: 🔄 **Mistake Bank Review + 10-Minute Mock Test**

### Step 2: Play Exactly ONE 10-Question Mission
- In the **"🗺️ Adventure Missions"** tab, tap today's subject.
- Click **Set 1** (or the next unlocked set).
- Every set is **strictly 10 questions** (never mixed), keeping cognitive load comfortable for a 7-year-old.
- As questions are answered, instant feedback appears:
  - ✅ **Correct**: Emeralds clink, confetti pops, and Steve cheers!
  - 💡 **Incorrect**: A friendly growth-mindset message appears ("*Mistakes help our brain grow!*") along with a crystal-clear explanation. The question is **automatically saved into the Mistake Bank** for later.

### Step 3: Use the Voice "🔊 Read" Button
- If a word is tricky to read (like *thermometer*, *quadrilateral*, or *chameleon*), your child can tap the **"🔊 Read"** button next to any question.
- The computer will read the question and all 4 options out loud in clear speech.

## 🎯 Weekend Prep: How to Use the SOF Exam Simulator
About 2–3 weeks before the real Olympiad, switch to the **"⏱️ SOF Exam Simulator"** tab:
1. **Quick 10-Q Mock**: Perfect for getting used to the TCS iON question grid (Green = Answered, Purple = Marked for Review, Grey = Not Visited).
2. **Full 35-Q Official Mock**: 60-minute countdown with exact SOF section weights (Logical Reasoning, Everyday Mathematics/Science, Achievers Section HOTS).
3. Teach your child the golden SOF strategy:
   - *Use the purple "Mark for Review" button if a question is tough, move ahead, and come back at the end!*

## 📲 Installing as an iPad App (PWA)
Once, with internet on:
1. Open the site in **Safari** on the iPad.
2. Tap **Share → Add to Home Screen → Add**. An emerald "SOF Quest" icon appears.
3. Open it from the Home Screen — it launches full-screen, like a real app.
4. Open every subject tab once (Adventure, Exam, Mistake Bank) so the offline cache fills. After that, daily practice works with **no internet**.

Notes: read-aloud needs one tap first (iPad rule — any button tap counts). If a new version ever looks stuck, remove the Home Screen icon and re-add it.

---

## 🔄 Resetting for a Fresh Start
If you ever want your child to practice all 300 questions again from scratch:
1. Tap the **"👨‍👩‍👦 Guide"** button in the top right.
2. Click **"Reset All Progress for Fresh Start"**.
3. Type `RESET` and confirm.
